# Leo Cozy Crew — Ep. 1 "The Magic Family Band" — Generation Prompt Pack

Format: 9:16, 1080x1920, 30 s total = 6 clips x 5 s (image-to-video / reference-to-video).
Use ref_leo.jpg + ref_family.jpg as character references on EVERY clip.
Workflow: 1) make one keyframe image per clip with all refs attached, 2) animate each keyframe (5 s),
3) generate voices separately (or native audio if the model supports it), 4) edit + burn subtitles_en.srt.

Exactly five characters, 3D Pixar-style toddler-friendly cartoon, identical to references:
LEO — toddler boy, big brown eyes, short brown hair, red baseball cap with white star, blue pajamas with yellow stars, white shoes.
MOM — ADULT young woman (mother, not a child), long dark curly hair in a ponytail, yellow long-sleeve top, pink skirt with white flowers, pink shoes, golden tambourine.

## CLIP 1 (0–5 s) — Morning
Leo in his cozy bedroom, sitting up in a small bed, stretching and yawning, then hears music, smiles, climbs down and toddles toward the open door with warm light. Slow push-in, then camera follows him.
Line (Leo, cheerful toddler voice): "Good morning! What's that music?"

## CLIP 2 (5–10 s) — Surprise!
Leo peeks into the living room through a doorway. Reveal: Mom shaking tambourine, Dad strumming guitar, Grandma ringing golden bells, Grandpa drumming. Mom turns to Leo with open arms. Camera over Leo's shoulder, gentle dolly forward.
Lines: Mom (warm young woman): "Good morning, Leo! Come and dance!"  Dad (friendly man): "Let's play together!"

## CLIP 3 (10–15 s) — Let's dance (Leo + Mom)
Leo bouncing and dancing in the center of the rug, arms waving; Mom dances beside him shaking the tambourine. Medium shot, low toddler-height camera, gentle orbit.
Line (Leo): "Yay! This is so much fun!"

## CLIP 4 (15–20 s) — Instruments
Quick cozy close-ups feel in one shot: camera pans across Dad strumming guitar, Grandma ringing bells and smiling at Leo, Grandpa drumming a fun rhythm; Leo dancing in foreground.
Line (Grandma, sweet elderly voice): "Wonderful, Leo!"

## CLIP 5 (20–25 s) — Family music (wide)
Beautiful wide cinematic shot of all five performing together in the living room, Leo clapping and dancing in the middle, everyone laughing. Slow crane-up.
Line (Grandpa, jolly elderly voice): "Music makes us happy!"

## CLIP 6 (25–30 s) — Happy ending
All five stand side by side facing camera (Grandpa, Grandma, Leo in front center, Mom, Dad), smiling and waving at the viewer. Static frontal shot, warm glow, sparkles.
Line (everyone together): "Leo Cozy Crew! We love our family!"

## AUDIO
Original cheerful kids' music, ukulele/acoustic guitar + tambourine + handbells + small drum, ~110 BPM, light and bouncy; duck music under dialogue.
